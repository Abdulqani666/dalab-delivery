## Apple App Store

**Name (max 30):** Dalab Delivery

**Subtitle (max 30):** Fast moto delivery to you

**Promotional text (max 170):**
Order from local shops and markets. A moto rider picks it up and brings it to your door. Follow your rider live on the map.

**Description (max 4000):**
Dalab Delivery brings the shops and markets near you to your door by moto.

How it works
- Choose a shop: food, supermarket, electronics, clothes, stationery, gas and fuel, and more.
- Add what you want to your cart.
- Drop a pin on the map where we should deliver.
- A rider accepts your order, collects it, and brings it to you.
- Follow your rider live on the map and call the rider if needed.

Why customers like it
- Fast, reliable and on time.
- The delivery fee is shown before you order.
- Pay cash when your order arrives.
- See all your orders and their status in one place.

Dalab Delivery is made for local shops and the people around them.
