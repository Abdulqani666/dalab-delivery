Dalab Delivery Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Dalab Delivery is a moto delivery app. Customers order from local shops and markets, and a rider delivers the order. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who is responsible

The Dalab Delivery service, run from Puntland, Somalia. You can contact us at: [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

2. What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Your name and phone numberWhen you create an accountTo log you in, and so the rider can call you about your order
Your passwordWhen you create an accountTo log you in. We never see it in plain text; it is stored protected
Your delivery location (the pin on the map)When you place an orderSo the rider knows where to deliver
Your phone's current locationIf you allow it, when you set your delivery pinTo place the pin near you. You can refuse and tap the map instead
Order details: shop, items, prices, note to the rider, status, timeWhen you orderTo prepare, deliver and show your order history
Rider locationOnly for riders, while a rider has an active order and the app is openSo the customer can see the moto on the map

We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers. We do not use the app for advertising or tracking across other apps and websites.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account and log you in.
  • To process and deliver your orders, and to show you where your order is.
  • To let the shop owner and riders contact you about an order.
  • To fix problems and keep the service safe.

We do not sell your information.

4. Who can see it

  • Riders and Dalab Delivery staff can see your name, phone number, delivery pin and order details for orders they handle.
  • Customers can see the name, phone number and live location of the rider assigned to their active order.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (secure database and login, servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and Google Maps (map display). They process data only to provide those services.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

5. Location

Location is used only when you choose to use a feature that needs it (setting your delivery pin, or a rider delivering an order). The app asks your permission first. You can turn it off in your phone settings at any time. The app does not track your location in the background.

6. Payment

Payment is cash on delivery. The app does not collect bank or card details.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and order history while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and personal information. We may keep basic order records that we need to settle accounts or to meet legal duties.

8. Your choices

  • Ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct it.
  • Ask us to delete your account and information. Send your request to the email address above from the phone number you registered with, or use the account deletion option in the app when it is available.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in a database with access rules, so customers can only see their own orders. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a password that others cannot guess.

10. Children

Dalab Delivery is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app.
